**Overview :**
This research project explores the numerical and simulation-based analysis of warpage in Fused Deposition Modeling (FDM) 3D printing, particularly in fiber-reinforced Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene (ABS) materials.
Warpage is a critical issue affecting the geometric accuracy of printed components, and this study aims to investigate its causes and mitigation strategies.

**Objectives :**
Analyze the effects of geometric parameters (length, width, height, and layer thickness) on warpage.
Use simulation models and statistical techniques to understand warpage behavior.
Develop insights to optimize FDM printing parameters for better dimensional accuracy.
Contribute to improving additive manufacturing processes, particularly for fiber-reinforced composites.
